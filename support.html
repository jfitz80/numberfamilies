Number Families Support

A few answers to keep your learning journey moving.

Getting Started

Complete the first guided world to learn how arrays represent multiplication. Later worlds introduce related facts, growing arrays and turning grids.

Restore Full Access

Open the purchase screen and choose “Restore Purchases.” Make sure the device is signed into the Apple Account originally used for the purchase.

Sound

Use the Speak button inside a lesson to hear supported instructions. Check the device volume if no sound plays.

Progress

Progress is saved on the device. Deleting the app may remove locally saved lesson progress.
