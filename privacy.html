Number Families Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026

About Number Families

Number Families is an educational multiplication app. The app does not require an account.

Children’s personal information

The developer does not knowingly collect names, email addresses, precise locations, photographs, contacts or other personal information from children.

Learning progress and preferences

Learning progress and preferences are stored locally on the device.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive complete payment-card information.

Advertising and tracking

The app does not display third-party advertising. The app does not sell personal information. The app does not use third-party behavioural tracking.

Apple’s services

Apple’s services remain subject to Apple’s own privacy policy.

Privacy questions

A parent or guardian may contact the developer at numberfamilieshelp@gmail.com with privacy questions.

Please do not include a child’s name, school, photographs or other personal information.
